Finding Martha's Vineyard

Century Communities describes Martha's Vineyard as a gated community of more than 100 acres in Alvin. Its sales office is at 401 Merlot Drive, and the builder places the community just northwest of the intersection of State Highway 6 and County Road 46, minutes from Pearland. Streets carry wine names, including Merlot, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Cabernet.

Two large lakes, a playground and a splash pad are listed as planned amenities. Both new construction and resale homes appear in listings. Gated entry matters when you call for help, so tell the provider the gate code situation before they arrive.

Typical Reasons Homes Here Get Wet

Air conditioner drain overflow

Summer humidity keeps air handlers running, and a clogged drain line drips into ceilings.

Plumbing fittings

Crimped or loose connections can leak inside a wall long before a stain shows.

Tub and toilet overflows

A stuck valve or clog can put an inch of water across a bathroom and into the room below.

Read about AC leak water damage if the stain is under an air handler, or ceiling water damage repair if a bathroom upstairs is the source.

What to Do While You Wait

  1. Stop the source. Close the fixture valve or the main water valve.
  2. Do not step into standing water near outlets, appliances or extension cords.
  3. Lift furniture legs off wet carpet and place foil or plastic under them.
  4. Photograph the ceiling, walls and floor, and the source of the leak.

Drying and Documentation

Moisture meters show what the eye cannot. Ceilings that look dry can hold water above the drywall, and walls can stay wet behind baseboards. A written record of readings at the start and end of drying is the best proof that the structure was dried properly.

Insurance adjusters often ask for photos and a scope of work. Ask for both. Neither one proves coverage, because coverage depends on how the water got in and on the policy. According to the IICRC S500 standard, drying is verified with meter readings compared against dry reference materials.

Gate and Access Planning

A gated entrance adds one step to every visit. Tell the provider whether the gate is staffed, whether it needs a code and who can open it if you are not home. Write down the nearest cross street as well as the house number.

If you are away when a leak is found by a neighbor or a monitoring service, give the provider and your insurer a contact who can let people in. Water that runs for a weekend can move from one room to the whole first floor.

Drainage and Flood Information

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. We did not find a map or a news report that covers this community's drainage, and we are not saying it floods. Martha's Vineyard Water Damage Questions

Is Martha's Vineyard in Alvin?

Century Communities lists it with an Alvin address in ZIP 77511, northwest of State Highway 6 and County Road 46.

Does the gate slow help down?

It can. Give the provider your address, the gate instructions and a phone number that will be answered.

A stain appeared under my air handler. What now?

Turn off the air conditioner, clear the drain line if you know how, and call. Wet insulation above the ceiling needs to be checked.

Should I call my insurer first?

You can do both. Report the loss, keep the damaged materials and ask the provider for photos and readings.

Is every ZIP 77511 address the same flood risk?

No. Risk changes street by street, so verify your own lot on the FEMA map.
