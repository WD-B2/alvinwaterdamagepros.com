Carpet Water Damage Restoration in Alvin, TX

Carpet looks dry long before it is. The pad underneath holds most of the water, and that is where mold and odors start.

Carpet is a two-part system. The surface fibers sit on a foam or fiber pad, and the pad sits on the subfloor. Water goes through the carpet and collects in the pad, which acts like a sponge. Drying the top of the carpet with fans does not dry the pad.

Clean Water, Gray Water and Black Water

Whether wet carpet can be saved depends on the water that wet it.

Water typeSourceCarpet outcome
Clean (Category 1).Supply line, rain through a roof.Often dried in place if caught within a day or two.
Gray (Category 2).Washer or dishwasher discharge.Carpet may be cleaned. Pad is usually replaced.
Black (Category 3).Sewage, flood water.Carpet and pad are removed.

Time matters as much as water type. Clean water left for several days turns into a damp, bacteria-friendly environment.

What to Do With Wet Carpet

  1. Stop the source and remove furniture. Wood and metal legs stain carpet.
  2. Blot standing water with towels. Do not walk on it more than necessary.
  3. Do not use a household vacuum.
  4. Open windows only if the weather is dry. In a humid season keep them closed and run the AC.
  5. Photograph the area before lifting anything.
  6. Call us before you pull up carpet so the pad and subfloor can be measured.

Our Carpet Drying Process

  1. Extract

    Water is pulled from the carpet and pad using extraction tools.

  2. Lift and inspect

    Carpet is detached and lifted so the pad and subfloor can be checked.

  3. Dry

    Air movers dry the carpet and subfloor. Dehumidifiers pull moisture from the room.

  4. Replace if needed

    If the pad is soaked or contaminated, it is removed. Carpet that cannot be dried is replaced.

Wet Carpet and Humid Gulf Coast Air

In Alvin the air is humid for much of the year. Wet carpet in warm, humid air can grow mold within a few days. That makes quick extraction and dehumidification more important here than in a dry climate. A musty odor means moisture is still present.

If water ran across a large part of the house, water extraction explains the first step. If the carpet is in a lower level, see basement water damage cleanup.

The Pad Decides the Outcome

Carpet manufacturers and cleaners agree that the pad is the weak point in a wet floor. Foam pad can absorb a great deal of water and hold it against the subfloor, and it is difficult to dry in place. For that reason a technician usually pulls back the carpet at a corner and checks the pad and the tack strip.

If the pad is wet but the carpet itself is clean, the usual fix is to remove and replace the pad, dry the subfloor and reinstall the carpet. This often costs less than replacing the whole carpet and keeps the colors matching.

Dealing With Odors and Stains

A sour smell after carpet dries means moisture or bacteria stayed in the pad or backing. Treating the carpet with deodorizers masks the smell but does not remove the cause. If the smell comes back on humid days, there is probably still moisture below.

  • Brown stains that wick up from the backing mean dirt from the pad has moved into the fibers.
  • Delamination, where the backing separates from the carpet, means the carpet has been wet for too long.
  • Rust spots around tack strips point to long exposure to moisture.
  • Rippling means the stretch has been lost and the carpet needs a power stretch after drying.

Area Rugs and Furniture

Lift rugs off wet carpet as soon as possible. Dyes can transfer to the carpet beneath. Wood furniture legs leave stains in damp carpet, so place foil or plastic tabs under each leg. Move upholstered pieces to a dry room and open them to the air. Keep a list of anything that was in contact with water for your insurer.

The industry reference behind carpet water damage work is the IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, which we use as the yardstick when explaining drying goals and equipment.

Carpet Questions

Do you restore wet carpet in Alvin?

Yes. Call (844) 950-6900 and describe the water and the age of the carpet.

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often yes if the water was clean and it is caught quickly. Contaminated water means replacement.

Why replace the pad?

Pad holds water and is hard to dry. It is inexpensive compared to the damage it can cause if left wet.

Will the carpet shrink?

Sometimes. A professional stretch after drying can fix it.

Does insurance cover carpet?

Sudden water damage often is. Ask your agent.
