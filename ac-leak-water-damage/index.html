AC Leak Water Damage Repair in Alvin, TX

In Alvin's humid summers an air conditioner makes a lot of water. When the drain line clogs, that water goes into the ceiling and attic insulation.

An air conditioner pulls moisture out of the air as it cools it. That water, called condensate, drains through a small line to the outside. In Alvin, where summers are hot and humid, a system can produce gallons a day. If the line clogs with algae and sludge, the drain pan fills and overflows.

Signs of an AC Leak

Ceiling stain near a vent or hallway

Water from an attic air handler often appears in a hallway or closet ceiling.

Water around the indoor unit

A pool near a closet unit points to a full pan or a cracked pan.

Musty smell when the AC runs

Damp insulation and ductwork give off an odor that grows stronger when the system starts.

Ice on the coil

A frozen coil melts into the pan faster than it can drain.

What to Do When the AC Is Leaking

  1. Turn the system off at the thermostat and at the breaker.
  2. Look for a puddle around the indoor unit and in the pan if you can reach it safely.
  3. Remove standing water with a wet vacuum.
  4. Do not walk in an attic you cannot see in. Stay on joists and watch for wet insulation.
  5. Take photos of the unit, the pan and any stains.
  6. Call an HVAC technician to clear the line and call us to dry the wet materials.

How Cleanup Works

Water from a condensate line is normally clean at first, but standing water in a pan can grow algae, so crews treat wet material accordingly. Attic insulation soaks up water and holds it against the ceiling drywall. Removing and replacing the wet insulation is usually necessary, and the ceiling is dried from above and below.

  1. Check the extent

    Moisture meters trace the water from the unit to every wet ceiling, wall or floor.

  2. Remove wet insulation

    Soaked batts and blown insulation are removed from the attic.

  3. Dry the structure

    Fans and dehumidifiers dry the joists, drywall and any flooring.

  4. Document

    Photos and readings are kept for your claim.

Why Alvin Systems Are Prone to Overflows

Alvin summers run hot and humid, and AC units work most of the year. A system that runs constantly produces constant condensate, and the line never gets a rest. Algae builds in the line a little every month. A line that was only slightly slow in May may be blocked by August.

A float switch in the pan can shut the system down when water rises. Many homes do not have one. Annual maintenance that flushes the line is the cheapest way to prevent this loss. If the water reached the ceiling and walls, see ceiling water damage repair.

Insurance for AC Leaks

Texas homeowners policies often treat an overflow as sudden and accidental when it happened at one time. Long-term leaks from a drain that dripped for months are usually excluded as gradual. Document dates and any maintenance records.

Attic Air Handlers and Hidden Damage

Many Gulf Coast homes have the air handler in the attic. When the pan overflows there, water soaks the insulation and the ceiling drywall below. The first sign may be a stain in a hallway or a closet. By then, the insulation has been wet for some time.

An attic is hot, and moisture trapped there can lead to mold in a short time. After an overflow, the cavity should be opened, the wet insulation removed and the joists dried.

Maintenance Habits That Prevent Overflows

  • Have the drain line flushed each spring before the cooling season.
  • Pour a cup of white vinegar down the line every few months if your technician advises it.
  • Install a float switch that shuts the system off when water rises in the pan.
  • Check the pan and drain line visually before summer.
  • Change filters on schedule, since a dirty filter can freeze the coil.

Who clears the drain line?

Can an AC leak cause mold?

It can if wet insulation and drywall stay damp. Fast drying prevents it.

Is the water dirty?

Usually it is clean condensate, but standing water in a pan can carry algae. Wet materials are handled with care.

How can I prevent a repeat?

Schedule annual line cleaning, install a float switch and check the pan during summer.
