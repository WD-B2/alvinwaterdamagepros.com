What Fails in Newer Manvel Homes

PEX and fitting failures

Many newer homes use PEX plumbing. Crimped fittings and kinked lines can leak inside walls or attics.

Attic AC drain overflows

Air handlers in attics drain through a small line. When it clogs, water soaks insulation and the ceiling below.

Slab-on-grade leaks

Lines under the slab can leak and send water into flooring in any room.

Roof and window leaks

Wind-driven rain during storms finds gaps at flashing and window seals.

If the leak came from the air conditioner, read about AC leak water damage. For a split pipe inside the wall, see burst pipe water damage.

If Water Is Still Spreading, What to Do First

  • Shut off the main water valve, or the valve at the fixture that is leaking.
  • Turn off breakers to wet rooms and stay out of standing water near outlets.
  • Lift furniture off wet floors and move valuables to a dry room.
  • Photograph the damage, then call the 24/7 line and give your Manvel address.

Flood Risk Around Manvel

Manvel was settled along Chocolate Bayou, and southern Brazoria County is flood-prone. Whether your home is in a flood zone is something to confirm for your own address. The county's floodplain administrator and the FEMA flood map will tell you. Standard homeowners policies generally exclude flood, so ask your agent about a separate policy.

After the Water Is Out: Preventing Mold in Manvel Homes

Manvel's warm, humid climate means damp materials can grow mold within days. The best protection is fast drying. Keep the air conditioning on and the windows closed while equipment runs. Do not turn off dehumidifiers overnight. If a musty smell remains after the equipment leaves, tell the technician, because it can mean a wet cavity was missed.

Texas licenses mold assessors and remediators, and the same company generally may not do both on one project. If visible growth appears, our mold remediation page explains the steps and the certificate that follows.

Documenting a Loss for Your Insurer

  • Photograph the source and each wet room before cleanup.
  • Keep damaged items until the adjuster has seen them, or photograph them first.
  • Save receipts for emergency repairs and equipment.
  • Write down the date and time the damage was discovered.
  • Report the loss promptly, since some policies set deadlines.
