The Historic Center of Alvin

Downtown Alvin is centered near West Sealy Street, North Hood Street and Gordon Street, which is State Highway 35. The Alvin Depot, now a museum, and murals on brick buildings at Sealy and Hood mark the area. Stanton's, a local convenience store, has operated there since 1922, according to the Houston-Galveston Area Council's 2024 Alvin Livable Centers Study.

Residential streets around downtown include Sidnor Street, Parkview Street near Sealy Park and the blocks around Adame Park, which the study describes as nearly all residential with few sidewalks. The study reports 49 percent owner occupancy in its area, against 57 percent citywide, so rental homes are common.

Documented Flood and Drainage Facts

The same study reports that nearly the entire western half of its study area lies within a mapped flood zone. It also says the eastern section was among the areas hit hardest by Hurricane Harvey. Mustang Bayou acts as a natural barrier, and ditches and drainage dips affect access to parks such as Talmadge Park.

These facts describe parts of the study area, not every address. Zone and elevation vary by lot, so check your own address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center. Flood status for individual downtown streets is not verified here.

Flood water is contaminated. Water that came from a street, ditch or bayou can carry sewage and chemicals. Keep out of it, turn off power to the area if you can do so safely, and read the flood damage cleanup page before you go back in.

Homes and Storefronts

Older buildings often mix finished and unfinished spaces, so water finds hidden routes. A storefront can have a wet back room and a dry front. Commercial owners should read the commercial water damage page for how business losses are handled.

If cleanup could disturb old paint, flooring or insulation, ask a professional whether testing is needed first. Do not scrape or sand suspect materials yourself.

What to Do First After Water Enters a Downtown Building

  • Do not wade through water that came from the street or a ditch.
  • Turn off the main breaker only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without standing in water.
  • Photograph the water line on walls before it drops.
  • Move paperwork, electronics and keepsakes above the water line.
  • Call a professional for sewage, gas smells or any water that reached outlets.

Cleanup, Step by Step

  1. Make it safe

    Confirm power, gas and structural conditions before anyone works in the room.

  2. Remove the water

    Pumps and extractors take out standing water, and wet-vac work follows.

  3. Remove what cannot dry

    Porous materials soaked by contaminated water are bagged and removed.

  4. Dry, clean and document

    Equipment runs under daily readings, and surfaces are cleaned and photographed.

Sewage backups follow different rules. See sewage backup cleanup if a floor drain or toilet is the source.

Insurance After a Downtown Loss

Standard homeowners policies generally exclude flood, according to the Texas Department of Insurance. A separate flood policy is needed for water that rises from outside. Ask your agent whether you have one before the next storm. Photos, receipts and a written scope help any claim, and they never mean a claim will be paid.

Downtown Alvin Questions

What are the boundaries of Downtown Alvin?

There is no official boundary we could verify. We describe it as the area around Sealy, Hood and Gordon Streets near the Alvin Depot.

Is Downtown Alvin in a flood zone?

Part of the area studied by the Houston-Galveston Area Council is in a mapped zone. Your street may or may not be, so check the FEMA map.

Did Hurricane Harvey flood downtown?

The planning study says the eastern section was among the areas most severely affected. It does not count homes or streets.

Can I clean up bayou water myself?

Small areas of clean water can be handled by owners. Water from a street, ditch or bayou should go to a professional.

Are storefronts handled too?

Commercial water damage is one of the listed services. Describe the space and the damage when you call.
