About Kendall Lakes

Kendall Lakes sits in Alvin and is served by Alvin ISD. Builder sites place it along State Highway 35, with entry from Kendall Lakes Boulevard and homes on streets such as Kendall Crest Drive and Latigo Court. One builder describes three lakes with water views and greenbelts for walking.

The homes are mostly recent single-family construction. Lennar lists plans from about 1,600 to 3,200 square feet with three to five bedrooms. A resale listing in the community shows a home built in 2018. The lakes are part of the neighborhood setting, and no source we found says they flood homes.

Where Indoor Water Damage Starts

Most calls in a community like this begin inside the house rather than at the lake. These sources are the usual suspects in any two-story home.

Upstairs leaks

A supply line, toilet or tub overflow upstairs can soak a ceiling and run down into the room below.

Water heater and AC drain

Tank failures and clogged air handler drain lines put water in closets and attics.

Kitchen and laundry

A dishwasher hose, refrigerator line or washer hose can leak behind cabinets for days.

Each of these has its own page. Start with ceiling water damage repair when water comes through from above, or water heater leak cleanup when the tank is the source.

Before Anyone Starts Cleanup

  • Keep people and pets out of rooms with standing water if outlets or cords are nearby.
  • Shut off the water at the fixture valve or the main shutoff, but only if you can reach it safely.
  • Do not use ordinary fans or a household vacuum on wet floors.
  • Photograph the damage and the source before moving anything.
  • Call a professional if water is spreading, or if it came from a drain or sewer line.

What Cleanup Looks Like

  1. Find and stop the source

    The first job is confirming the leak is off and mapping which rooms are wet.

  2. Extract the water

    Pumps and extraction units remove standing water and pull water from carpet and pad.

  3. Dry the structure

    Air movers and dehumidifiers run while moisture readings are logged until the framing is dry.

  4. Repair what was removed

    Wet drywall and insulation that cannot be dried are cut out and replaced.

Drying is the step homeowners underestimate. The structural drying page explains the equipment, the readings and why drying finishes before any new drywall goes up. According to the IICRC S500 standard, drying goals are set by comparing wet materials with dry readings from the same home.

Flood Maps and the Lakes

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. We did not find a source that maps this neighborhood's flood zone or describes past flooding here. Check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, and ask the city or Brazoria County floodplain administrator what applies to your street.

Alvin as a whole has a real flood history. The National Weather Service recorded 33.60 inches of rain at a site 1.6 miles southwest of Alvin during Hurricane Harvey in 2017. That is a regional record, not a statement about this neighborhood. If rain or flood water entered the house, read about flood damage cleanup before you re-enter.

Insurance and Documentation

Whether a loss is covered depends on the cause and on your policy. Texas homeowners policies usually cover sudden and accidental discharge from plumbing and often exclude flood. Ask your insurer before you decide anything. Photos, moisture readings and a written scope of work help describe what happened and what was done, and they do not mean a claim will be paid.

Kendall Lakes Water Damage Questions

Which part of Alvin is Kendall Lakes in?

Builder sites place Kendall Lakes along State Highway 35 in Alvin, ZIP 77511, with access from Kendall Lakes Boulevard. Give the street name when you call so the address can be confirmed.

Do the lakes make flooding likely?

We found no source that says so. Flood-zone status for the neighborhood is not verified, so check the FEMA map for your own address.

What should I do first after a leak?

Stop the water at the shutoff if you can reach it safely, stay away from wet outlets, photograph the damage and call for help.

Is a newer home less likely to leak?

Not necessarily. Fittings, hoses, water heaters and air conditioner drains fail at any age, and water can hide behind finished walls.

Can I dry the house myself?

Small, clean spills on hard floors can be handled by a homeowner. Wet carpet, wall cavities and ceilings usually need professional drying equipment.
