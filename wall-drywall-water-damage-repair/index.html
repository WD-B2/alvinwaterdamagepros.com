Wall and Drywall Water Damage Repair in Alvin, TX

Drywall absorbs water through its edges and paper face. Some wet walls dry in place, and others have to be cut out before they fail.

Drywall is gypsum between sheets of paper. When it gets wet, the paper soaks up water and the gypsum softens. If it dries quickly, it can recover. If it stays wet, it sags, stains, grows mold and eventually crumbles.

What to Do for a Wet Wall

  1. Stop the water source and turn off power to outlets in the wall.
  2. Move furniture away from wet walls.
  3. Remove wall hangings and photograph the stains.
  4. Do not paint over wet drywall or stains.
  5. Keep the AC running to reduce humidity.
  6. Call us for moisture readings before cutting.

How We Dry and Repair Walls

  1. Measure

    Meters and thermal cameras find wet areas, including behind baseboards and inside cavities.

  2. Open smartly

    A flood cut removes drywall a set height above the waterline. Insulation is removed.

  3. Dry the cavity

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the studs and plates.

  4. Rebuild

    Once readings are normal, new drywall is hung, finished and painted.

Wall Damage in Local Homes

Alvin homes often have drywall over wood framing on slabs. Water from a pipe, a roof or a storm travels into wall bottoms. Baseboards hide wet drywall. High humidity prolongs drying, which is why a closed house with working AC and dehumidifiers dries a wall faster than an open one.

The ceiling can be wet at the same time. See ceiling water damage repair for the overhead case. After heavy flooding, see flood damage cleanup.

Why Wet Walls Fail Quietly

A wall can hold water for a long time before anything shows. Water that enters at the bottom plate climbs the drywall by capillary action, a few inches at a time. Paint hides the early stages. Homeowners often notice only when a baseboard separates, a stain appears at knee height or the paint begins to bubble.

Inside the cavity, fiberglass insulation behaves like a wet sponge pressed against the back of the drywall. It keeps the paper face damp and slows drying from both sides. That is the main reason a wall that feels dry at the surface can still read wet on a meter. It is also the reason cutting a small inspection opening is often worth it.

Cutting Out Wet Drywall the Right Way

A clean cut line makes repair easier. Technicians mark a level line above the highest wet reading and cut along it, which leaves a straight edge for the new board. The cut lands on a stud where possible. The removed material is bagged so wet gypsum dust does not spread.

  • Remove baseboards first and number them so they can go back in order.
  • Cut at least a few inches above the highest moisture reading, not at the stain.
  • Pull wet insulation and set it aside for disposal.
  • Leave the cavity open while equipment runs so air can reach the studs and plates.
  • Check the bottom plate and the lowest part of each stud for staining or softness.

Once the studs read dry, new drywall is hung and finished. Skipping that check is the most common reason a repaired wall stains again within a season.

Paint, Primer and Finish After Drying

A dried wall that kept its shape can often be sealed with a stain-blocking primer and repainted. A water ring will bleed through ordinary paint, so primer matters. Where texture was disturbed, the patch needs to be blended into the surrounding finish. We keep the mitigation scope separate from the cosmetic scope so an insurer can see where drying ends and finishing begins.

The industry reference behind wall and drywall water damage work is the IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, which we use as the yardstick when explaining drying goals and equipment.

Wall Water Damage Questions

Do you repair wet drywall in Alvin?

Yes. Call (844) 950-6900 and describe the wall and the water source.

Can wet drywall dry in place?

Sometimes. Clean water caught fast may. Contaminated or long-wet drywall is removed.

What is a flood cut?

A horizontal cut above the waterline that removes wet drywall so the cavity can dry.

Will insurance cover it?

Sudden water damage is commonly covered. Ask your adjuster.

How long before I can repaint?

After the wall reaches normal readings, which usually takes several days.
