Alvin has a long record of flooding, and the cleanup that follows is heavier than a leak cleanup. Water that enters from outside crosses lawns, ditches and streets before it gets to your floor. That is why flood damage is treated as contaminated until proven otherwise.

Flooding Is a Known Risk Here

Brazoria County's hazard mitigation plan, adopted in 2018, puts Alvin's annual chance of flooding at 35 percent. It describes Mustang Bayou running along the edge of the city and notes that residential areas near West Talmage Road and Bellaire Boulevard sit in the 100-year floodplain. The plan counts 106 residential and 6 non-residential repetitive loss properties in Alvin. Those are properties that flooded more than once and filed claims.

The weather record backs it up. The National Weather Service reports Tropical Storm Claudette in July 1979 produced an official one-day rainfall of 27.75 inches at Alvin. In Harvey in 2017, a gauge 1.6 miles southwest of Alvin collected 33.60 inches. The county plan also lists 2001 flooding that put the Highway 35 underpass and South Johnson Street underwater, and 2002 flooding that reached homes.

What Makes Flood Water Different

Contamination

Flood water picks up sewage, fuel, fertilizer and soil. Porous materials that touched it are usually removed.

Mud and silt

Silt gets into wall cavities and under flooring. It has to be cleaned out, not just dried.

Structural saturation

Floor framing, wall bottoms and insulation can stay wet for weeks without proper drying.

Electrical hazards

Outlets, appliances and the panel can be energized or damaged under water.

If Water Is Still Coming In, Do This First

  1. Get everyone to higher ground and keep children and pets away from the water.
  2. Do not enter a flooded room if outlets or cords are underwater. Call the utility to cut service if the panel is wet.
  3. Move important papers, medications and electronics above the waterline.
  4. Photograph the waterline on the walls and the damage before cleanup. This record matters for flood insurance.
  5. Do not run a regular vacuum or use appliances that sat in water.
  6. Call the emergency line and tell the dispatcher how deep and how dirty the water is.

If you have a National Flood Insurance Program policy, the Texas Department of Insurance says the claim must be filed within 60 days. Report the loss early and keep damaged items for the adjuster.

How Flood Cleanup Works

  1. Make the site safe

    The crew checks for electrical and structural hazards and sets up protective gear.

  2. Pump and extract

    Deep water is pumped out, then floors are extracted and shoveled or washed clean of silt.

  3. Remove what cannot be saved

    Wet carpet, pad, insulation and contaminated drywall are cut out and bagged.

  4. Clean, sanitize, dry

    Surfaces are cleaned and treated, then structural drying equipment runs until readings are normal.

What Affects Flood Cleanup Cost

Cost follows depth, duration and contamination. A few inches over one room is a small job. Several feet in a whole house, with mud in the walls, is a rebuild. We explain the scope and the price before work starts, and we document the waterline and damage for your insurer. Flood losses are often handled under a separate policy from your homeowners coverage, so check which one applies.

Flood Questions

Is emergency flood cleanup available in Alvin?

Yes. Call (844) 950-6900 any hour. During a major storm many homes need help at once, so the dispatcher will prioritize active water and safety hazards and explain what is possible.

Does homeowners insurance cover flood damage?

Standard homeowners policies generally do not cover flood. You need a separate flood policy, such as one under the National Flood Insurance Program. Ask your agent which policy applies.

Can wet drywall be saved after a flood?

Usually not. Flood water is contaminated, so drywall that wicked it is typically cut out above the waterline.

How soon should cleanup start?

As soon as it is safe. Mold can begin growing within a few days in Alvin's warm, humid weather.

Do I need a mold test after a flood?

Not automatically. Texas licenses mold assessors separately, and testing is usually done by an independent assessor if growth is suspected. See our mold remediation page.
