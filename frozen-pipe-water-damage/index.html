Freezing weather is rare on the Texas Gulf Coast, which is exactly why it causes so much damage. Many homes here have supply lines in attics, exterior walls and garages with little insulation, because no one expects a hard freeze. When one comes, ice expands inside the pipe and splits it. Water usually does not pour out until the ice melts.

Why the Damage Shows Up After the Thaw

Ice acts as a plug. While the pipe is frozen, the split may be sealed and the house looks fine. As temperatures rise, the plug melts and water flows through the split under full line pressure. Homeowners often leave for work on a freezing morning and return to a flooded house by afternoon.

Low flow or no water

A faucet that trickles or runs dry in cold weather points to ice in the line.

Frost on exposed pipes

Visible frost on pipes in the garage, attic or under a sink means they have reached freezing temperatures.

Bulging or cracked pipe

A swollen section is a likely split. Do not thaw it with a flame.

Water after the thaw

Stains or drips appearing when temperatures rise suggest a split has opened.

What to Do When Pipes Thaw and Leak

  1. Close the main water valve before pipes thaw if you suspect a freeze. This limits the water that can escape.
  2. Open faucets slowly and look for drips, stains or sound from the walls.
  3. Never use an open flame, torch or kerosene heater to thaw a pipe.
  4. Turn off power to any wet room and keep out of standing water near outlets.
  5. Photograph split pipes, wet areas and damaged items for your insurer.
  6. Call a plumber to repair splits and call us for water removal and drying.

Our Cleanup Process After a Freeze

  1. Find every split

    Freeze damage often involves more than one break. The crew checks along the affected runs with moisture meters.

  2. Pull the water

    Extraction removes standing water. Wet insulation and soft drywall below the splits are cut out.

  3. Dry the cavities

    Air movers and dehumidifiers dry walls, ceilings and subfloors. Attic insulation may need replacement.

  4. Record the loss

    Moisture logs, photos and a scope of work document the damage for your carrier.

Freeze Losses on the Gulf Coast

National Weather Service storm data for February 2021 lists a cold and wind chill period from February 12 to 16 and a winter storm from February 13 to 15. It also lists extreme cold on February 15 and 16 and an ice storm on February 17. These entries cover the Houston, Brazoria and Galveston forecast zones. The data has no Alvin-specific figures, but it confirms the conditions that split Brazoria County pipes.

Attic lines are the most common failure point because insulation gets damp and settles. A split there wets the ceiling below it, so ceiling water damage repair often follows a freeze loss. Exterior hose bibs and lines in outside walls are the next most common.

Frozen Pipe Questions

Is emergency frozen pipe cleanup available in Alvin?

Yes. Call (844) 950-6900 at any hour. During widespread freezes demand is high, so the dispatcher will explain what can be done and when a crew may arrive.

Does insurance cover frozen pipe damage?

A split from a sudden freeze is commonly covered as sudden and accidental damage. Some policies expect reasonable steps to prevent freezing. Check your policy and report the loss quickly.

Can I thaw a pipe myself?

A hair dryer on low heat pointed at the pipe is safer than any flame. If the pipe is already split, thawing will release water, so shut off the main first.

What if I find damage days after the freeze?

Report it right away and document it. Water from a late-thawing split can be treated as sudden. Long-running seepage is treated differently by most policies.

How do I prevent this next winter?

Insulate attic and exterior wall lines, disconnect hoses, and know where the main valve is. If a hard freeze is forecast, let faucets drip.
