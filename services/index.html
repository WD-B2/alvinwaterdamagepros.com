What to Do First, Whatever the Service

  • Stop the source of water if you can do it safely.
  • Turn off power to wet rooms and stay out of standing water near outlets.
  • Photograph the damage before moving anything.
  • Call the 24/7 line and describe what is wet.

How to Choose Between Services

Start with the water source. Clean water from a supply line is handled differently from water that came up through a floor drain or across the yard. Contaminated water means porous materials such as carpet and drywall usually come out instead of being dried.

Next, look at how long the area has been wet. Anything wet for more than a day or two needs structural drying even if the surface looks fine, because moisture stays in framing and wall cavities. That is the reason structural drying and dehumidification sits alongside almost every other service.

Questions About Our Services

Do I need a separate company for repairs after drying?

Not necessarily. Mitigation, meaning extraction and drying, stops the damage. Repairs rebuild what was removed. We explain which parts of the scope are mitigation and which are repair, so you can see what your insurer is being asked to cover.

Why is mold listed last?

Mold is a possible result of water damage, not a starting point. Most calls begin with water, and quick drying often prevents mold. If growth already exists, the mold page explains Texas licensing and clearance.

Do you handle commercial properties?

Yes. Shops, offices and small warehouses in Alvin are covered on the commercial water damage page.
