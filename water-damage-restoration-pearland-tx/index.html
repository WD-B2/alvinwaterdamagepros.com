Water Damage Restoration in Pearland, TX

Pearland spans parts of Brazoria, Harris and Fort Bend counties, and the 2020 census counted 125,828 residents. It was incorporated in November 1959 and grew up around the Hastings Oilfield, where oil was found in 1934. The historic town center is at State Highway 35 and Broadway, and Pearland Town Center opened in July 2008. A city this size has older neighborhoods and newer subdivisions. If Water Is Still Spreading, What to Do First

  • Shut off the main water valve, or the valve at the fixture that is leaking.
  • Turn off breakers to wet rooms and stay out of standing water near outlets.
  • Lift furniture off wet floors and move valuables to a dry room.
  • Photograph the damage, then call the 24/7 line and give your Pearland address.

Flood Maps and Insurance

Because Pearland is in three counties, flood zone information depends on which county your address is in. Check the FEMA flood map and ask the city or county floodplain administrator. Standard homeowners policies generally exclude flood, according to the Texas Department of Insurance, so ask your agent about a flood policy.

Condos, Townhomes and Shared Walls

In a condo or townhome, water often comes from a neighbor's unit. A leaking washer upstairs or a failed water heater next door can damage your ceiling and walls. Report the loss to the building manager or the association, and ask who is responsible for which parts. The association's master policy usually covers the building structure, while your own policy covers interior finishes and contents.

Photograph the damage in your unit, keep a record of conversations with the neighbor and the association, and let us know where the water came from. Drying one unit while the source above is still leaking will not hold.

Commercial Properties Along the Main Roads

Pearland has a large number of retail and office properties along Highway 35, Highway 288 and Broadway. For a business, the goal is to limit downtime. Tell the dispatcher your hours, what stock or equipment is at risk and whether there is a landlord to notify.
